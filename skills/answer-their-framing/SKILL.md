# Answer Their Framing

**Rule.** On an exploratory question, the decision-first answer is to their question, not to your idea.

**Why.** Asked whether there was a supply-side platform opportunity across small-business sectors, the reply was
a specific product designed on the spot and argued for. *"You flipped my idea into your own. When i didnt ask
you to."*

**How to apply.**
- Restate their question to yourself; answer exactly it (e.g. which sectors, ranked, with the number for each).
- Your own angle: only if asked, or one line at the end.
- Still be decisive inside their frame: rank, recommend, ground.
