# The Brief's Numbers Are the Spec

**Rule.** Read for numbers and objects, not just intent. A coherent misreading doesn't feel like one.

**Why.** A brief held a one-week delay on *friends' replies reaching the author* and a two-week lock on *the
author's own update*. The build merged both into one seven-day hold on *the entry*, the object neither delay
was about. The group chat the brief called the whole point sat empty for a week, and the docs argued the wrong
version confidently. Caught only by quoting the product's own copy back: *"this was not the premise."*

**How to apply.**
- Make a table: every number in the brief → the object it acts on → "held from whom / applies to whom".
- Keep the brief verbatim as README; say in the repo's instructions it outranks every engineering doc. Re-read
  before changing behaviour.
- If the channel can't enforce the literal design, hand over the alternatives; don't silently pick the
  enforceable one.
