# Breadth After a Miss

**Rule.** A miss means the spread was too narrow. Widen it; don't polish the loser.

**Why.** After a fully built direction was rejected, the instinct is to iterate on it. But the rejection said the
premise was wrong, and polish can't fix a premise. The recovery that worked was a new page of genuinely
different premises shown small, then iterating on the one picked.

**How to apply.**
- Don't ask what was wrong with it if they gave a taste signal; you'll get a guess. Show alternatives.
- New round = new premises (vary-the-premise), shown small, then the finalists at full size.
- Keep what exists live meanwhile; a rejected rebuild never replaces a working original.
