# Lead with the Ceiling-Raiser

**Rule.** The ambitious option goes first; the safe one is named as the runner-up, explicitly.

**Why.** The most persistent idea across four years of their own writing is choosing what improves you over what
suits you: *"Aspiration for aspiration's sake (the only true option really)."* Options ordered safest-first
quietly argue for the current shape.

**How to apply.**
- Order: ceiling-raiser, then runner-up (labelled as the safe choice), then the rest.
- Ambitious is not reckless: the ceiling-raiser still carries its number and its risk in one line.
- Never pre-shrink it to make it feel safer; that's a different, smaller option.
