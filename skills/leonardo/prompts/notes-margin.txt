WRITING: the sheet carries handwritten notes in the same ink as the drawing, small and even, in blocks that wrap around the studies. This handwriting is ILLEGIBLE by design — it is made only of tiny looping and hooked pen-strokes that suggest a fifteenth-century Italian hand written backwards, and it must form no real word in any language. Not one readable word, letter-group, or number-word anywhere. Single letters (a, b, c, m, n) marking points on a diagram are allowed; ordinary Arabic numerals in a column of arithmetic are allowed.
