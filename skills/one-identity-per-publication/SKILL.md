# One Identity per Publication

**Rule.** Siblings share a maker, not a look.

**Why.** A second magazine was built with bone paper, ink and a single cobalt in its own three typefaces,
explicitly *"different from"* the first title's cream-and-vermilion. When a third was started, the second's
chart look was liked but the instruction was not to copy it exactly.

**How to apply.**
- Write the identity down at the start: paper, ink, one accent, display/body/mono faces.
- Check it against every sibling; if two titles could swap covers unnoticed, change one.
- Borrow a technique, never a whole look.
