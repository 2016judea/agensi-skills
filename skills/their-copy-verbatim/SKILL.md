# Use Their Copy Verbatim

**Rule.** Their roughness is the signal that a person wrote it. Polishing deletes the thing that works.

**Why.** A homepage draft was reworded, every step and bullet: *"you changed the language from my original
draft"* and *"No. I like mine. It's authentic."* It had to be restored word for word.

**How to apply.**
- Paste as-is: phrasing, structure, punctuation style, informality.
- Fix only clear misspellings of real words or proper nouns.
- If something seems genuinely wrong (a factual error, a broken sentence), ask; don't fix silently.
- Copy you author yourself is a different case; voice guidance applies there, not here.
