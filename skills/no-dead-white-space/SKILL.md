# No Dead White Space

**Rule.** Space must be composed. A page that reads unfinished is a bug.

**Why.** *"quite a bit of whitespace … shrink down by 15% in page count"*; *"a lot of white space on page 15"*;
*"page 8 can we add a black and white art plate?"*; *"shrink the [diagram] and put an art plate on the top
half"*. But a bottom-aligned quote alone on a page was asked for. The difference is intent.

**How to apply.**
- After pagination, scan every page for blocks of empty space bigger than about a quarter of the page.
- Fix in order: reflow/tighten (fewer pages), then a chosen art plate (art-is-chosen-not-filler), then resize
  the neighbouring figure.
- Leave space that is clearly placed: a lone quote, a section opener.
