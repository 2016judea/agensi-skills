# Inspect Every Page Before You Show It

**Rule.** Nothing visual reaches the person until you have looked at all of it, rendered.

**Why.** On a magazine build, the session that went well turned every correction into a rule the same minute.
What cost cycles was building before inspecting: defects the person found that a page-by-page look would have
caught first. They asked for visual inspection before anything reaches them.

**How to apply.**
- Render and screenshot every page/frame at the sizes it will be seen. Read the screenshots.
- Check what code can't: art the wrong way up, low resolution, repeated images, empty space, text that runs
  under art, contrast on the paper colour.
- Fix, re-render, re-look. Then show, with anything you couldn't fix stated up front.
