# Colour Tells the Story

**Rule.** Colour is read before any word. Make it carry the argument.

**Why.** On a house-value tool the ask was asking price in one colour, all evidence in a second, our estimate in a
third: *"use color theory to tell the story too… consider this in the website holistically."* Shipped: blue =
their asking price, sand = what the records say, green = what we think it's worth, amber = worth a closer look.

**How to apply.**
- List the roles numbers play on the page. One colour per role, at most four or five.
- Apply everywhere that role appears: columns, headers, cards, headline figures. Add a small swatch key.
- Brand accent is not a role colour. Check contrast for every role colour on its background.
