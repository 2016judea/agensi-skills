# Show the Quiet Option Too

**Rule.** Know their prior, but don't let it pick for them.

**Why.** The default taste is bold, editorial, high-contrast with one hot accent. Yet for print, the subtlest of six
back covers was chosen, and an antiquarian book design beat the brutalist one. A spread of only bold options
would have hidden the winner.

**How to apply.**
- Every spread includes at least one restrained direction, built as seriously as the bold ones.
- Print and screen are judged differently: paper rewards restraint.
- Don't rank the quiet one last by reflex; rank on the brief (rank-the-creative-set).
