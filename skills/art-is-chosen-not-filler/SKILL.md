# Art Is Chosen, Never Filler

**Rule.** A repeated or careless plate says nobody chose the art.

**Why.** An issue's ten art slots were filled by four works, so one plate ran four times: *"i see duplicate art
plates? no dupes please."* Also caught: a full-page diagram printed sideways, and a low-resolution scan
(*"we do not risk shipping a low resolution art"*).

**How to apply.**
- Count slots after pagination; source at least that many distinct works before building. A crop of a sheet
  that also runs full-page is the same work.
- Verify in the rendered output that no image source repeats. Re-check after any reflow; slots move.
- Everything from the writer's original posts goes back in, credited. Credits are bare and small, in a corner.
- Resolution gates layout: no plate larger than its pixels support. Check orientation on every full page.
