# No Gloss on Their Material

**Rule.** A screen carries (a) their own sentences verbatim, (b) the record's own fields (date, title, credit,
film stock, rating), and (c) nothing else.

**Why.** Struck one by one in an evening: a photo caption (*"its poor taste and anyone arriving at my site can see
right thru it"*), a "tap one to open it" line, a count of publications with "newest first", a tagline. *"I dont
like it when you add these UX guidance annotations. They are sloppy and unnecessary."* Later the same for
explanatory captions under every drawn plate in a magazine.

**How to apply.**
- If a section seems to need an explanatory line, it doesn't; the content is the explanation.
- No captions you wrote, no summaries, no counts, no usage hints, no "drawn for this issue" notes.
- Credits stay: bare, small, findable.
