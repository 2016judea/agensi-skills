# Interview, Don't Ghostwrite

**Rule.** Authorship is the artefact. A better draft doesn't fix a draft they didn't write.

**Why.** Three research pages built from someone's own internal design docs were good, and liked, and deleted
anyway: *"you are going to guide me through a series of prompts (written by you) so that i can write out
detailed explanations… so that when we are ready to publish, it is entirely my own writing."*

**How to apply.**
- Read the source material, then write the prompts: one specific question per section, in the order the page
  will run, asking for the concrete detail (numbers, what went wrong, what they'd tell a newcomer).
- They answer in their own words. You assemble, order and format; you don't rephrase.
- Your contribution is structure and questions, never sentences.
