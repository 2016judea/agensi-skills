# Find the Spread Before Drawing

**Rule.** If the drawing needs a label to make the difference visible, the difference isn't visible.

**Why.** A card compared two counties' rent burden, 29% against 30.5%: *"the data is too close and it immediately
loses the reader."* The comparison came from the data model (county vs county), not from where the gap was.
Swapping geography for time (working from home: 5% for a decade, then 31% in one year) gave a 4x story from the
same survey.

**How to apply.**
- Scan for the biggest honest spread: across time, places, groups, before/after. Pick the axis that has it.
- Say the ratio out loud. Under ~1.5x, find another axis or use words, not a chart.
- At tiny volumes (1 vs 0), a bar chart lies with axes; write the sentence instead.
