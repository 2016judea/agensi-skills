# Check the Premise, Not Adoption

**Rule.** Data checks the premise. Shipping checks adoption. Don't swap them.

**Why.** *"When we do business brainstorming and prototyping i want you to be data-based… fact-check our
suspicions."* And separately, on adoption guesses: *"We have fallen for this trip far too many times"*: a
confident model of the customer kept standing in for evidence of use.

**How to apply.**
- Every idea carries its fact-check: the count, rate or spread, and the query it came from. "Lots of X" is not
  a number.
- A number that kills the hunch is reported in the same breath.
- Never write "they'll use it because…" or "they won't, because…". Ship with analytics on and read real use.
