# Rank the Creative Set

**Rule.** The ranking is the deliverable. Unranked options push the judgement back onto the person.

**Why.** Four plates from a comic-panel base were delivered ranked, with #3 called out for drifting into
blacklight-poster violet and #4 named weakest (modern digital comic, not 1977, halftone gone). The whole
reply was *"love it."* Agreement that costs nothing is worth nothing; that applies to grading your own output.

**How to apply.**
- Look at every frame first (inspect-before-you-show).
- Order by how well each served the brief. One line each: what it got right, what it got wrong.
- Say where the engine, not the brief, chose the palette: it tells them whether a re-run fixes it or it's a new
  direction.
- Don't soften the weak ones. Encode rank in the filename (01-, 02-…).
- End on the single highest-value next move (e.g. one re-run with the drift capped).
