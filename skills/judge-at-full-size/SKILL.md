# Judge at Full Size Before the Pick

**Rule.** Get the expensive direction to full size on the cheap side of the choice, not after it.

**Why.** A contact-sheet homepage was chosen at 340px wide. The identical idea, built at full size with real
material, was rejected. The thumbnail had validated nothing.

**How to apply.**
- Small previews are for narrowing a wide spread. The final two go to full size, real copy, real images, before
  the pick.
- Live prototypes (an artifact or a throwaway page) beat static mockups, because feel is the thing being judged.
- If only one can be built full size, build the riskiest one; the safe one is easier to imagine.
