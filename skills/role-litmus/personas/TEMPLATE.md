# <Label>

> Sections 1-4 are written **before** this persona is shown anything of your product.

## 1 · Who
The job, the employer type, team size, constraints they talk about. No invented name, age or
hobbies. Cite a source line for each claim (a permalink, a public post, a job listing).

## 2 · Top tasks, in their own words
3-5 tasks, ranked. Each with a short paraphrase of how they describe it and a link to where they
said it. Task 1 is what pass 2 walks.

## 3 · What they use today, and what it costs
The **named incumbent** (tool, vendor, spreadsheet, a person they call), its price, and what
they complain about. Pass 4 runs against this, never against nothing.

## 4 · Vocabulary
The words they use for the things your product does. Pass 1 asks "what is this?" in these words.

## 5 · What they've been publicly burned by
Bad data, vendor lock-in, a tool that lied. This is where their anxiety comes from.

## 6 · What they will not do
Account walls, a second subscription, anything that needs code, data with no update date,
anything their compliance team forbids. Each with a reason.

## 7 · Their disqualifier
The single thing that ends the evaluation immediately.
